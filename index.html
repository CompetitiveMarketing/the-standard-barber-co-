05 / Good To Know

QUESTIONS?
SORTED.

A few things worth knowing before you pull up a chair. These policies are examples for the demo.

Do I need to book ahead?

Booking ahead is recommended to secure your preferred time. Walk-in availability depends on the day.

How long does a haircut take?

Most standard cuts take around 30–45 minutes. Detailed fades and combined services may take longer.

Can I choose my barber?

Absolutely. Select your preferred barber when booking, subject to availability.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give as much notice as possible. Cancellation terms will be confirmed by the actual business.
